Privacy Policy

Vaultium is designed to help you manage your expenses securely and privately.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the information you provide while using the app, including your registered account details, expense records, categories, and support messages.

2. How We Use It

Your data is used only to provide dashboard analytics, budget tracking, yearly history reporting, and customer support services related to your account.

3. Security

We protect your financial records by keeping them tied to your authenticated user account and by storing them in the app's local database.

4. Contact

If you have concerns about privacy or account data, please use the Help & Support page to contact us.

This privacy policy may be updated over time as the application evolves.
